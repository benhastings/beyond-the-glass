By the end you'll be able to…

Five things you can do at a real keyboard, on a real bad night.

  • Explain what a container image is and trace one from a registry to a running pod.
  • Triage a failing pod with kubectl get, describe and logs --previous, and name the root cause.
  • Read an Argo CD application: tell synced from drifted, healthy from degraded, and find what changed.
  • Follow one slow request from a Grafana panel to its trace in Tempo and its log lines in Loki.
  • Work a multi-failure incident on a microservice from first alert to a written root cause.
