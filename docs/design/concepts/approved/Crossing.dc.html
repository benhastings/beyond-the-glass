Storyboard · plays once, after “Step through the glass”

The Crossing

2.8 seconds from the Console Room to the Reach. One continuous camera move, one slow swell of light, no flashes. The learner can skip from the first frame, and anyone who prefers reduced motion gets a 400 ms crossfade instead.

  1. 1 · Push in

    0.0 s → 0.9 s

    The camera scales from 1.0 to 1.6 toward the middle monitor, ease-in. Room light dims by a third as the glass brightens. The Skip button is present and focused from the first frame, and stays in this corner throughout.

  2. 2 · Ripple

    0.9 s → 1.9 s

    Five rings expand from the center, each 120 ms behind the last, and the glass brightens in one continuous swell. Brightness rises once and never pulses, so nothing flashes. Console text dissolves from the edges inward.

  3. 3 · Arrival

    1.9 s → 2.8 s

    The bezel falls away and the Reach resolves, ease-out. Two faint rings linger at the edge of the view, then fade. The journey map controls fade in over the last 300 ms and focus moves to the heading “The Reach”.

Timing

Arrival · 900 ms
  • Total 2.8 s, under the 3 s ceiling. Push in 0.0–0.9 s, ripple 0.9–1.9 s, arrival 1.9–2.8 s.
  • Skip: a real button, focused on entry, at least 48 px tall. Enter, Space or Escape jumps straight to the map with focus on its heading.
  • Plays the first time only. Later visits open the map directly; a “Replay the crossing” link lives in Settings.
  • No sound, no flashes, and no content that depends on having watched it.

Reduced-motion alternative

400 mscrossfade

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, or the in-app motion setting turned off, the Console Room crossfades to the Reach in 400 ms. No scaling, no rings, no camera move. Skip is still offered.
