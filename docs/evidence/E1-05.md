## Self-check against `docs/design/art-direction.md`, for the reviewer

- **World map** (`src/app/art/WorldMap.tsx`): the approved concept (`Reach.dc.html`), regrouped so each region is
  one `<g id="map-region-<content region ID>">`, plus ground, hills, shore and trail layers. A focused pin
  highlights its region and dims the others to 45 % (an opacity transition, which reduced motion allows).
  The pins sit just below their landmarks, so they don't cover the art.
- **Vignettes** (`src/app/art/vignettes.tsx`): one 4:1 cut-paper band per region, drawn here (the concept has
  them only as parts of the map), in each region's motif colours from the art direction table, with at most
  six colours each:
  - Console Room: the beige CRT with the glowing glass, and status lights
  - Harbor: deep water, stacked containers in coral, gold and mist, a dockside crane
  - Helm Keep: the violet keep with a gold pennant, and distant towers (one arrow slit, so the keep's
    windows and door don't read as a face)
  - Chartroom: the brass dome, a compass rose, a chart on warm paper
  - Mirror Lake: the shore reflected in still water, with ripple rings (drift)
  - Signalwood: layered trees, with gold firefly signals passing between them
  - Stormwatch: a watchtower on a cliff under indigo cloud, one lit window, one stroke of lightning (static:
    nothing flashes)
- **Never drawn:** no people, faces or silhouettes, no animals or creatures, no logos, no real places or
  maps, nothing from a fantasy franchise.
- All of it is decorative (`aria-hidden`). The map's links and the list view carry the places and their
  status in text, and each region page names its region in its heading.
