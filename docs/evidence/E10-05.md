# E10-05 evidence

`E10-05.AC3` (tone review: blameless and encouraging) needs an SME's sign-off. Until it's added as an
`## E10-05.AC3` section here, the AC gate reports the criterion as untraced, on purpose.

## Tone self-review, for the AC3 reviewer

What the debrief says, and the rules it was written to:

- **No grades or numbers that read as a grade.** "What went well" says, for example, "You looked before you
  changed anything in 3 of 4 acts." No percentages or points (tested), no pass or fail, and time isn't
  mentioned except as the clock in the timeline.
- **Blameless.** Each act says what caused it and what each signal showed. The learner's part is "Looked
  here" or "Didn’t look here", not "missed". Risky commands come under "Worth knowing next time", each with
  the reason it's risky, not a judgement of the learner.
- **Encouraging, and only true.** "What went well" lists only what happened (from the scores). When
  nothing qualifies, it says "You stayed with the incident to the end. Everything else grows from that."
- **Banned words** (simply, just, obviously, easy, trivial) don't appear (tested, along with "score" and
  "grade").
- **Notes.** With no notes, it says "You didn’t keep notes this time. They help the next person, and the
  debrief after." The example note sits beside the learner's own.

To sign off, add a level-2 heading with the criterion's tag (E10-05.AC3) at the end of this file, and under it
who reviewed it, when, and the outcome, including any changes asked for.
