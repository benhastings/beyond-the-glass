## Self-check against `docs/design/art-direction.md`, for the reviewer

- **Source:** the scene is the approved Console Room concept (`docs/design/concepts/approved/Main.dc.html`),
  converted to a React SVG component (`src/app/art/ConsoleRoomScene.tsx`). It adds one thing the issue
  asks for and the concept didn't have: a narrow window with a night sky, a crescent moon and four stars,
  at the left edge.
- **Style:** flat, layered cut-paper shapes. The only gradients are the wall, the night sky, the sky inside
  the glass and the glow behind it. Colours are the Console Room motifs (`#7DE8D8`, `#EFE9DC`, `#FFB84D`,
  `#6FE0A0`), the beige plastic and the mood's ground.
- **Never drawn:** no people, hands or silhouettes, no animals, no product logos, no real places, nothing
  from a fantasy franchise.
- **Motion:** status lights blink three times (0.8 Hz), the side CRTs dim by 10 % at 2 Hz, and the glass
  brightens once and rests at 85 %. All of it, start delays included, ends within five seconds. With
  reduced motion, from the OS or the in-app setting, nothing moves. Nothing flashes.
- **Layers** for the Crossing (E1-06): `back`, `desk`, `monitor-left`, `monitor-right`, `glass`, `props`.
