<Step id="terms" title="Terms as you meet them">

New words are links. Focus or hover one, such as <Term id="context">context</Term>, and its plain-words
meaning appears; follow it, and the Field Guide opens at that term with the operator's definition and
related terms. Press <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> to close a preview.

The Field Guide is in the header on every page, with a search box that knows aliases and acronyms, so
"OOM" finds "OOMKilled".

</Step>

<Step id="depth" title="Choose your depth">

Most ideas come in layers. Pick the one that suits you; the lesson remembers your choice for the whole
topic, and you can change it at any time.

<Depth>
  <ELI5>
    This is the plain-words layer. Short sentences. One idea at a time. Good for a first look.
  </ELI5>
  <Operator>
    This is the operator's layer: what the thing does, which command shows it, and how it compares
    with what you ran before. Most of the course is written for this layer.
  </Operator>
  <DeepDive>
    This is the deep dive: how it works underneath, for when you want to know why, or you're the one
    debugging the odd case.
  </DeepDive>
</Depth>

Where you start is set by the Threshold, the placement quiz. You can always go up or down a layer.

</Step>
