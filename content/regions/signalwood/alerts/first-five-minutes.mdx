<Step id="sequence" title="Understanding before fixing">

Five minutes, in this order:

1. **Acknowledge** the page, and say you're on it.
2. **Assess the impact**: who, how many, since when.
3. **Check what changed** recently: `argocd app history`. A deploy is the usual suspect.
4. **Read the dashboards**: which service went first.
5. **Open a trace**: which span, on which pod.
6. **Read that service's logs.**
7. **Check the pods and events** with kubectl (after confirming the cluster).

<Callout kind="habit">
  All four habits in five minutes: check your context, look before you touch, correlate signals, and
  change through Git when you do act.
</Callout>
