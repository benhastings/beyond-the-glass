<Step id="rules" title="An alert is a query with a threshold">

<Rosetta id="nagios-alert-rule" />

An <Term id="alert-rule">alert rule</Term> is a PromQL query, a threshold, and a `for` duration: it fires
when the query has been over the threshold for that long. Each rule has a severity (**page** wakes someone;
**ticket** waits for the morning) and a <Term id="runbook">runbook</Term> that says where to start.

</Step>

<Step id="for" title="Why `for`?">

<Depth>
  <ELI5>
    The alert waits a few minutes before it rings. If the problem goes away by itself in that time,
    nobody gets woken up for nothing.
  </ELI5>
  <Operator>
    While the condition holds but `for` hasn't passed, the alert is **pending**; then it's
    **firing**. Short blips never page. Too long a `for`, and you hear late; too short, and you're
    woken for noise. Pages are usually 2–5 minutes.
  </Operator>
</Depth>

</Step>

<Step id="read" title="Read the page">

Ravenpost pages on symptoms at the gate, where senders meet the service. A page there says users are
affected; it doesn't say which service behind the gate is to blame.
