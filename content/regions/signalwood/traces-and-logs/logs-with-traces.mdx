<Step id="structured" title="Structured logs">

<Rosetta id="tail-logs" />

Ravenpost's services log JSON: one object per line, with named fields. A line written while serving a
request carries that request's `trace_id` and `span_id`, so a log line can take you to its trace, and a
span can take you to the lines written while it ran.

<CodeBlock
  language="json"
  caption="One line from the ledger"
  code={`{"level":"info","ts":"2026-03-14T20:41:33Z","msg":"request","method":"POST","path":"/entries","status":200,"duration_ms":612,"trace_id":"a7c8c4adf2e3451e7ffff92200000000","span_id":"812b3f5a5930c19c"}`}
/>

</Step>

<Step id="logql" title="Finding lines">

<CodeBlock
  language="logql"
  caption="The ledger's lines, then only its warnings"
  code={`{namespace="ravenpost", app="ledger"}
{namespace="ravenpost", app="ledger"} | json | level="warn"`}
/>

`{…}` picks streams by label; `|= "text"` keeps lines containing text; `| json` parses each line so you can
filter on its fields.

</Step>
