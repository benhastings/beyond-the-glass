<Step id="path" title="Panel, exemplar, trace, span, logs">

Five hops, each one click in the workspace below:

1. The **p99 latency** panel shows it got slow, and when.
2. Its **slowest sampled requests** (exemplars) link to their traces.
3. In the **trace**, the widest span with little below it is where the time went.
4. The span names the **service and operation**.
5. **Logs for this span**, or that service's logs from when it got slow, say why.

<Callout kind="habit">
  Correlate signals. Metrics say when and how much; traces say where; logs say why.
</Callout>
