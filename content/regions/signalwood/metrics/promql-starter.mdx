<Step id="rate" title="rate()">

A counter only goes up, so ask how fast: `rate()` gives the per-second increase over a window, and copes
with counters that reset when a process restarts.

<CodeBlock
  language="promql"
  caption="Requests per second, per series, over five minutes"
  code={`rate(http_server_requests_total[5m])`}
/>

</Step>

<Step id="sum" title="sum by">

`rate()` returns one series per label combination: per pod, per status code, per route. `sum by (label)`
adds them up, keeping only the labels you name.

<CodeBlock
  language="promql"
  caption="Requests per second, per service"
  code={`sum by (service) (rate(http_server_requests_total[5m]))`}
/>

<CodeBlock
  language="promql"
  caption="The share of requests that fail, per service"
  code={`sum by (service) (rate(http_server_requests_total{http_response_status_code=~"5.."}[5m])) / sum by (service) (rate(http_server_requests_total[5m]))`}
/>

</Step>

<Step id="quantile" title="histogram_quantile()">

<Depth>
  <ELI5>
    The buckets count how many requests were faster than each line. The quantile finds the line that
    99 out of 100 requests beat. That's p99.
  </ELI5>
  <Operator>
    Take the rate of the buckets, sum them keeping `le` (and any label you want per-series), then
    ask for the quantile. Drop `le` and the function has nothing to work with; keep `pod` and you
    get a percentile per pod, which is rarely what you want.
  </Operator>
</Depth>

<CodeBlock
  language="promql"
  caption="p99 latency, across everything"
  code={`histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by (le) (rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_bucket[5m])))`}
/>

</Step>
