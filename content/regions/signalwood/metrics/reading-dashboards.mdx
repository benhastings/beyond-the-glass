<Step id="range" title="Time ranges">

A dashboard shows one time range. Too short, and the start of the trouble is off the left edge; too long,
and a sharp change is squashed flat. Start with the last hour; widen it until the line looks normal at the
left.

</Step>

<Step id="annotations" title="What changed?">

**Annotations** are vertical markers for events, here every Argo CD sync: when it happened, and which
revision went out. The first question on any graph that changed is _what changed shortly before?_ A deploy
annotation a minute or two before the turn is the strongest lead you'll get.

<Callout kind="habit">
  Correlate signals. A spike on its own says something is wrong; a spike right after a deploy says
  where to look.
</Callout>

Every panel has **View as table**, with the same numbers and annotations as text.

</Step>
